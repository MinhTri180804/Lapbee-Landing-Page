Primary action control — use for CTAs ("Đặt lịch sửa", "Gọi ngay") and form submits.
```jsx
<Button iconRight="arrow-right">Đặt lịch sửa chữa</Button>
<Button variant="outline" iconLeft="phone">0909 000 000</Button>
```
- Variants: primary (bee yellow, max one per view), secondary (ink), outline, ghost, inverse (on ink sections).
- Sizes sm 36 / md 44 / lg 52px. Radius 4px. Press = 1px drop.
